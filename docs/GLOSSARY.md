# Nonstop vocabulary

This glossary covers real-life football concepts, simulation vocabulary, and narrowly authorized Fantasy Turn terminology. Other Fantasy Football vocabulary is deferred.

## Real-life football vocabulary

**American Football**:
The sport whose concepts include plays, downs, possession, formations, and penalties.
_Avoid_: NFL when referring to the sport generally.

**NFL**:
The National Football League, a professional American football league.
_Avoid_: NFL as a name for Nonstop or for American Football generally.

### Organizations and participants

**NFL Franchise**:
An enduring NFL organization whose identity continues as its players and coaches change.
_Avoid_: NFL Team when referring specifically to the organization.

**NFL Team**:
The football participants of an NFL Franchise considered collectively.
_Avoid_: NFL Franchise when referring specifically to the football participants; Roster as a synonym for the team.

**NFL Player**:
A person who plays football for an NFL Team. Within this project's scope, every NFL Player belongs to a team at all times, and a change of team does not change the player's identity.

**Roster**:
All players belonging to an NFL Team, regardless of their eligibility for a particular NFL Game.
_Avoid_: Team, franchise.

**Game Roster**:
The players on an NFL Team's Roster who are eligible to participate in a particular NFL Game.

**Position**:
A player's football classification, such as quarterback or running back. Play-specific assignments are outside this project's vocabulary scope.

**Depth Chart**:
An ordering of players at each Position indicating preferred starters and replacements. It expresses preference rather than identifying the actual On-field Players.

**Injury**:
A physical condition that prevents an NFL Player from participating in an NFL Game, within this project's scope.

### Competition

**NFL Season**:
The NFL regular-season competition, excluding preseason and postseason within this project's scope.
_Equivalent_: NFL Regular Season.

**NFL Week**:
A numbered round of the NFL Season's schedule, rather than a fixed seven-day calendar interval.

**NFL Week Schedule**:
The collection of NFL Games assigned to an NFL Week.

**NFL Game**:
A scheduled football contest between two NFL Teams. Its identity continues before play begins, during play, and after completion.

**Game Result**:
The final score and outcome of an NFL Game.

**Score**:
A team's accumulated points in an NFL Game.

**Touchdown**:
A scoring outcome in which a team legally possesses the ball in the opponent's end zone.

**Field Goal**:
The successful scoring outcome of a Field Goal Attempt.

**Extra Point**:
The successful one-point scoring outcome of an Extra Point Attempt.

**Two-Point Conversion**:
The successful two-point scoring outcome of a Two-Point Attempt.

**Safety**:
A scoring outcome that awards points to a team's opponent when that team is responsible for the ball becoming dead behind its own goal line, as determined by the Game Rules.

**Box Score**:
A summary of team and player statistics for an NFL Game, including its Game Result.
_Avoid_: Play-by-Play when referring to the statistical summary.

**Play-by-Play**:
The chronological record of Plays and Play Outcomes in an NFL Game.
_Avoid_: Box Score when referring to the chronological record.

### Plays

**Play**:
An individual football action within an NFL Game.

**Play Outcome**:
The result of a Play, including its football consequences and any effects of Flags and Penalties on which results count.

**Pass Play**:
A Play involving an attempted forward pass.

**Run Play**:
A Play in which the offense attempts to advance the ball by carrying it rather than attempting a forward pass.

**Kickoff**:
A kick that puts the ball into play at the start of a half or after a score, as prescribed by the Game Rules.

**Punt**:
A Play in which a player drops the ball and kicks it before it touches the ground, typically to send it downfield to the opposing team.

**Field Goal Attempt**:
A Play in which a team attempts to score a field goal by kicking the ball through the opponent's uprights and above the crossbar.

**Extra Point Attempt**:
A kick attempt after a touchdown to score one additional point.

**Two-Point Attempt**:
An attempt after a touchdown to score two additional points by advancing the ball into the opponent's end zone.

**Kneel-down**:
A Play in which the player holding the ball deliberately kneels to end the play, typically to consume game time.

**Spike**:
A Play in which the passer deliberately throws the ball into the ground immediately after receiving the snap to stop the Game Clock.

**Drive**:
One team's offensive series within an NFL Game.
_Avoid_: Possession as a synonym for the offensive series.

**Possession**:
Control of the football by a player or team.
_Avoid_: Drive when referring to ball control.

**Interception**:
A catch of an opponent's forward pass that gives the catching team Possession.

**Fumble**:
A player's loss of control of the ball other than by passing, kicking, or handing it off. A Fumble does not by itself establish a change of team Possession.

**Fumble Recovery**:
A player gaining control of a fumbled ball, whether for the team that fumbled or its opponent.

**Turnover on Downs**:
A transfer of Possession to the opponent when the offense exhausts its available downs without earning a new set of downs or scoring.

**Offense**:
The group of players participating in offensive play for an NFL Team.

**Defense**:
The group of players participating in defensive play for an NFL Team.

**Special Teams**:
The groups of players participating in kicking plays for an NFL Team.

**On-field Players**:
The players participating for one team in a play. Offensive Players and Defensive Players distinguish the two sides.
_Avoid_: Personnel, formation when referring to the participating players.

**Formation**:
The number of On-field Players at each position for one team in a play. Within this project's scope, Formation excludes the players' exact locations on the field.
_Avoid_: Position Mix, Position Composition, Personnel as alternative names for this concept.

### Game situation and rules

**Game Situation**:
The current competitive circumstances of an NFL Game, including Score, Possession, Down, Yards to Go, Field Position, time remaining, and Remaining Team Timeouts.

**Down**:
The numbered attempt in an offensive series of downs.

**Yards to Go**:
The distance needed to earn a new set of downs or reach the goal line.
_Avoid_: Distance without qualification.

**Game Rules**:
The rules governing an NFL Game, including scoring, timing, Overtime, and Penalty enforcement.

**Game Clock**:
The measure of time remaining in the current Quarter or Overtime of an NFL Game.
_Avoid_: Game Clock when referring to hurry-up strategy or other clock-management decisions.

**Timeout**:
An interruption of play in an NFL Game.

**Team Timeout**:
A Timeout charged against a team's allowance under the Game Rules.

**Remaining Team Timeouts**:
The unused portion of a team's Team Timeout allowance at a particular point in an NFL Game.

**Quarter**:
One of the four timed segments of regulation play in an NFL Game.

**Overtime**:
Play after regulation in an NFL Game, when applicable.
_Avoid_: Fifth quarter.

**Field Position**:
The ball's location along the football field.

**Flag**:
A rule violation in an NFL Game, within this project's vocabulary.
_Avoid_: Foul.

**Penalty**:
The prescribed consequence of a Flag, distinct from whether that consequence is accepted, declined, or otherwise resolved.
_Avoid_: Flag when referring to the consequence rather than the violation.

## Simulation vocabulary

**User**:
The human operating Nonstop.
_Avoid_: Player when referring to the human operator.

**Simulator**:
The Nonstop software.
_Avoid_: NFL, Simulated League as names for the software.

**Simulated League**:
An independently progressing football world with its own teams, players, schedule, and results, scoped to one Season.
_Equivalent_: SimLeague; League when the simulation context is clear.
_Avoid_: Simulation as the name for this world; NFL League.

**Team**:
The simulated counterpart of an NFL Team within a Simulated League.
_Equivalent_: SimTeam, Simulated Team.
_Avoid_: NFL Team when referring to a simulated entity.

**Player**:
The simulated counterpart of an NFL Player within a Simulated League, evolving independently from its real-life reference during that League.
_Equivalent_: SimPlayer, Simulated Player.
_Avoid_: NFL Player when referring to a simulated entity.

**Game**:
The simulated counterpart of an NFL Game within a Simulated League.
_Equivalent_: SimGame, Simulated Game.
_Avoid_: NFL Game when referring to a simulated entity.

**Simulate Game**:
The operation that produces a Game's results and Play-by-Play from beginning to end in one pass.

**Player Attributes**:
A Player's football characteristics used in simulation, distinct from recorded performance.

**Player Statistics**:
The recorded measures of a Player's performance in Games.
_Avoid_: Player Attributes when referring to recorded performance.

**Reference Data**:
Football information used as the starting basis for Simulated Leagues. Later changes to Reference Data do not automatically change existing Leagues.

**Franchise**:
The simulated counterpart of an NFL Franchise within a Simulated League.
_Equivalent_: SimFranchise, Simulated Franchise.

**Season**:
The simulated counterpart of an NFL Season, covering regular-season competition within a Simulated League.
_Equivalent_: SimSeason, Simulated Season, Regular Season.

**Week**:
A numbered round of a Season's schedule within a Simulated League.
_Equivalent_: SimWeek, Simulated Week.

**Week Number**:
The fixed number identifying a particular Week within its Season, independent of League progression or which Week the User is viewing.

**Current Week**:
The Week the League has reached through progression, initially Week 1. It remains current after Week Completion until the User advances the League.
_Avoid_: Next unplayed Week as a synonym.

**Previous Week**:
The Week immediately preceding the League's Current Week within its Season.

**Next Week**:
The Week immediately following the League's Current Week within its Season, absent when Current Week is the final scheduled Week.

**Advance Week**:
The User action that changes the League's Current Week to its Next Week, permitted only after Current Week Completion.

**Week Schedule**:
The collection of Games assigned to a Week within a Simulated League.
_Equivalent_: SimWeekSchedule, Simulated Week Schedule.

**Week Completion**:
The point at which every Game in a Week Schedule has finished and its results and Play-by-Play have been recorded. Completion does not itself advance the League to another Week.

**Season Completion**:
The point at which every Week in a Season has reached Week Completion, without an additional Advance Week action.

**League Completion**:
The point at which a Simulated League's required activities are finished and further progression is disabled.

**Completed League**:
A Simulated League that has reached League Completion, with its results and Play-by-Play still available for inspection.

**SimLeague End of Season Report**:
The final presentation of a Simulated League's accumulated results.
_Avoid_: League Summary.

**League State**:
The complete current circumstances of a Simulated League, including its progress, teams, players, and accumulated results.

**Snapshot**:
A fixed, preserved record of League State at a particular point in its progress, unchanged by later League progression.
_Avoid_: Save as a noun for this record; League as a synonym for its Snapshot.

**Current Snapshot**:
The latest saved League State permitted for restoration. Earlier Snapshots are not playable restore points after a Week has been completed.

**Save**:
The action of preserving a Snapshot of League State.
_Avoid_: Save as a noun for the preserved record.

**Autosave**:
Automatic preservation of a Snapshot as a League progresses, including after completion of a Week.

**Restore**:
Making a Snapshot's League State current, without necessarily advancing the League.

**Resume**:
Continuation of a Simulated League from restored League State.

**Game State**:
The complete current circumstances of a Game, including its progress, accumulated Play-by-Play, and Game Situation where applicable.
_Avoid_: Game Situation as a synonym for the complete Game State.

## Fantasy Turn vocabulary

**Turn**:
A stage within a Week against which permitted User actions are defined, with an action potentially available across multiple Turns. Each Week progresses through Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, and Weekend, independently of real-world weekdays or elapsed time.
_Avoid_: Week as a synonym for Turn.

**Current Turn**:
The League's active stage within its Current Week, independent of which Week or results the User is viewing.

**Advance Turn**:
The User action that ends the Current Turn and enters the next stage. Monday through Friday advance within the same Week; advancing from Weekend to the following Monday is also Advance Week.

**Weekend**:
The Turn during which all Games in the Week Schedule are played, combining Saturday and Sunday in the weekday analogy.
